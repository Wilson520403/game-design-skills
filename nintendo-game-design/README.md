## 📖 Overview

Nintendo’s timeless track record (*Super Mario*, *The Legend of Zelda*, *Pokémon*, *Super Smash Bros.*, *Splatoon*) is anchored in a unique, battle-tested design philosophy and engineering craftsmanship.

This Skill turns Nintendo's four-decade legacy of design wisdom into a structured AI mentor capability that supports:

1. **Toy-First & Core Verb Co-Design**: Start from a singular physical verb to craft a tactile "toy" before adding systems, applying Gunpei Yokoi's *Lateral Thinking with Withered Technology* and Miyamoto's *One Idea Solves Multiple Problems*.
2. **Kishōtenketsu (起承转合) 4-Step Level Design**: Introduce mechanic (Introduction) → Deepen safely (Development) → Unexpected twist (Twist) → Culmination & Mastery (Conclusion).
3. **Triangle Rule & Hakoniwa Exploration**: Drive curiosity in 3D / open-world spaces through visual occlusion, 3-tier landmark gravity, and compact miniature garden (箱庭) topology.
4. **Game Feel, Juice & Combat Micro-Feedback**: Coyote time, input buffering, asymmetric gravity jumps, Sakurai's 6 Hitstop factors, squash & stretch, and forgiving hitboxes.
5. **Textless "Invisible Tutorials"**: Adhere to "Show, Don't Tell; Play, Don't Read" using environmental affordances, safe-failure zones, and intuitive framing.

## 🎯 How to Use

Once installed, prompt your AI naturally in any game design conversation:

- **Toy Prototyping**: *"I want to make a game centered around 'Magnetic Grappling'. Help me apply Miyamoto's Multi-Solution Rule to make this single mechanic handle traversal, combat, and puzzle solving."*
- **Level Pacing**: *"Design a 3D platformer level around 'Flipping Gravity' using Koichi Hayashida's Kishōtenketsu 4-step structure."*
- **Game Feel Polish**: *"Our action platformer feels floaty and attacks lack impact. Give me concrete parameters for jump gravity, coyote time, and hitstop frames."*
- **Invisible Onboarding**: *"Audit our tutorial level to eliminate text popups and replace them with Super Mario World 1-1 style affordances and safe-fail setups."*
